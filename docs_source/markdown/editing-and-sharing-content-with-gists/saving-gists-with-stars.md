## Starring a gist

You can star a gist to add it to the "Starred" page. Starring makes it easy to find a gist again later.

1. Navigate to  and select the gist you want to star.
1. To star the gist, click ** Star** in the top-right corner of the page.

   ![Screenshot of the gist bar with the "Star" option highlighted with a dark orange outline.](/assets/images/help/gist/starring-a-gist.png)

1. Optionally, to unstar a previously starred gist, click ** Unstar**. This will remove the gist from your starred list.
